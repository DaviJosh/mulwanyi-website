Our Story

Why Mulwanyi International exists

Mulwanyi International was born from my passion for construction and my desire to contribute to the development of my community. As someone who has trained and worked in the construction industry, I have seen both the opportunities and challenges people face when trying to access quality, affordable construction services. This inspired me to build a company that combines professionalism, quality workmanship and affordability.

Our goal is to serve individuals, families, businesses, property developers and communities by providing reliable construction and real estate solutions that people can trust. I want Mulwanyi International to grow beyond just a construction company — to become a company that creates jobs, develops communities and turns people's dreams of owning quality property into reality.

The vision is simple: build with integrity, deliver quality and make construction accessible to more people.

Mulwanyi David Joshua
Founder, Mulwanyi International
